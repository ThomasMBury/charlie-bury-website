Charlie Bury is a UK-based screenwriter and script consultant dedicated to exploring the complexities of the human experience, primarily through a neurodivergent and theological lens.

Charlie tends to reject rigid formulas and predictable clichés. Instead, he combines a meticulous, analytical approach to cinematic structure with a passion for visceral, character-driven narratives. Whether crafting a satirical folk-horror feature, a historical epic, or a sharp TV comedy, his work confronts existential and universal themes with clarity, precision, and visual flair.

Currently, Charlie has an original television project in active development with producer Michael Graf at On The Hill Pictures.

Beyond his own slate, Charlie operates a dedicated script consultancy, bringing this same rigorous analytical and creative zeal to helping fellow writers and producers elevate their material for the commercial marketplace.

<p><strong>Charlie Bury</strong> is a UK-based screenwriter crafting character-driven drama, high-concept genre, and emotionally intense cinema.</p>

<p>Drawn to high stakes and visceral visual storytelling, his voice ranges from biting satirical folk-horror to sweeping biblical epics and razor-sharp, emotionally charged comedy-drama.</p>

<p>Charlie currently has an original television project in active development with producer Michael Graf at On The Hill Pictures.</p>

<p>Backed by a prolific work ethic, he maintains an active writer’s slate spanning feature specs, original pilots, and series bibles. The screenplays featured on this website represent only a curated preview -- Charlie maintains an extensive portfolio of developed concepts, treatments, and scripts across multiple genres, readily available for discussion with prospective agents, managers, and production companies.</p>

<p>Alongside his writing, Charlie takes on select <a href="/consulting/" style="color: #8B0000; font-weight: bold; text-decoration: underline;">script consultancy & story editing</a> projects, collaborating with writers and creative teams to hone narratives ready for the marketplace.</p>

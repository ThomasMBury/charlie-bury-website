Charlie Bury is a UK-based screenwriter crafting character-driven drama, high-concept genre, and emotionally intense cinema.

Drawn to high stakes and visceral visual storytelling, his voice ranges from biting satirical folk-horror to sweeping biblical epics and razor-sharp, emotionally charged comedy-drama.

Charlie currently has an original television project in active development with producer Michael Graf at On The Hill Pictures.

Backed by a prolific work ethic, he maintains an active writer’s slate spanning feature specs, original pilots, and series bibles. The screenplays featured on this website represent only a curated preview -- Charlie has an extensive slate of developed concepts, treatments, and scripts across multiple genres, readily available for discussion with prospective agents, managers, and production companies.

Alongside developing his own slate, Charlie works as a script consultant, collaborating with writers and creative teams to hone stories ready for the marketplace.

## ARANEA
*Feature Film | Satirical Folk-Horror*

Days after his mother’s suspicious suicide, a hyper-sensitive 10-year-old boy arrives at his wealthy uncle’s Devonshire estate for a shooting weekend - where he becomes the willing vessel for an ancient arachnid entity reborn from her corpse, helping it spin his abusive family’s doom.

## BLOOD REBELLION
*Feature Film | Dystopian Horror-Odyssey*

In a scorched European wasteland where daylight supercharges the undead, a devout young Benedictine monk must cross 2,000 miles of dead earth alongside his militant aunt and a cynical brother-in-arms to recover a sacred relic that holds the key to reclaiming the sun and saving humanity.

*Informed by lived experience.*
